How the survey scores are worked out
  • Each survey response rates one person on seven behaviors from 1 to 5. A response's average is the mean of its seven ratings.
  • A person's score in a period is the mean of all responses about them in that period. Team figures use every response in the period.
  • Periods follow the survey's Coverage field. The survey runs at the end of each month, so responses sent in November count toward October 2026.
  • Agents see team figures and their own scores, feedback and scorecard. Team leads see everyone's, except other team leads' feedback summaries. The administrator sees everything.
  • Evaluator names and email addresses are not part of this page.
